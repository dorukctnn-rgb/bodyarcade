Privacy

Last updated 7 October 2026

BodyArcade is made by Peak Apps Studio. This page explains, in plain language, what the game does with your data. If you have a question, email peakappsstudios@gmail.com.

Your camera

When you allow camera access, the video is processed in your browser by an on-device pose model (Google's MediaPipe Pose Landmarker, running through WebGL or WebAssembly). It turns each frame into body landmarks such as hips, shoulders and head, which the game uses to detect jumps, squats, ducks and leans. Camera frames are never uploaded, recorded or stored, by us or by anyone else. The camera is only on during the camera check and the run, and you can play without a camera using the keyboard.

The model files themselves are downloaded once from Google's and jsDelivr's content networks, the same way fonts or scripts are. Those networks see an ordinary file request from your IP address; they do not receive any video.

What is stored on your device

Your player name, body weight (used for the calorie estimate), chosen world and difficulty, run history, achievements, daily goal streak, free-run count and, if you have one, your Pro license key are kept in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data removes them. We can't see or recover them.

What is sent to other services

  • Pro license checks. If you activate Pro, the license key you paste is sent to Gumroad's license API to confirm the purchase, and re-checked in the background about once a day. Nothing else is sent with it.
  • Payments. Pro and Lifetime purchases are processed by Gumroad. We don't receive or store your card details. Gumroad's own privacy policy applies to the checkout and to the email it sends you.
  • Friend challenges. A challenge link contains the name you typed, your score, the world and the difficulty, encoded in the link itself. It goes only to whoever you send it to.

There is no online leaderboard, no live race server and no account. Scores are never uploaded.

Analytics

We use Plausible Analytics to count page views and a few actions, such as pressing Play or opening the Pro screen. Plausible doesn't use cookies and doesn't collect personal data.

Children

BodyArcade doesn't ask for accounts, email addresses or real names, and nothing you do in the game is shared unless you send a challenge link yourself. Younger players should have an adult check the room is safe to jump in.

Changes

If the way BodyArcade handles data changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
