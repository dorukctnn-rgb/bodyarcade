What is new

Last release 27 October 2026

What changed in BodyArcade, release by release, newest first. Pro includes the games we add later, and this is where they appear.

27 October 2026: Prices, release notes and small phones

  • The Pro screen fits small phones: the plans are on the first screen, and the buy button and “Continue free” share one row.
  • On a game’s page only Play stays at the foot of a phone’s screen; the races with a friend and with a ghost follow it in the page.
  • New pages: prices, these release notes, and a comparison with Active Arcade.
  • The home page says what BodyArcade is, which games have live races, where it plays and what Pro costs, also in a browser that does not run scripts.
  • The Pro screen lists the four workout plans of Pro and the games added later.
  • The body area pages count their own free games. They used to give the free games of the whole set.
  • bodyarcade.com/support opens the FAQ, and a visitor without an online profile no longer gets an error line in the browser console.

27 October 2026: Reaction Tag races and four guides

  • Reaction Tag can be raced live in a room: the same lights for everyone at the same moment. It is a Pro game, so the host needs Pro and the friends who join do not.
  • Four guides in English and in Turkish: fitness games, seated exercise games, games to play with friends and games by body area.
  • With a phone held sideways, the main button of Friends, the sign-up, Pro and Workout plans is on the first screen.
  • The trial reminder is a notice in the app, as the pages and the Pro screen now say.

26 October 2026: BodyArcade 2

  • 11 games, 8 of them free, for legs, arms, chest and back, core, cardio and balance. Every game has a seated version.
  • Live races with friends in rooms of 2 to 4 players, by an invite link or a code, with friends lists, public lobbies and ghost races.
  • Two players on one camera on a big screen, workout plans, a challenge of the day and weekly boards.
  • English and Turkish, and the plans of today: Pro monthly, Pro yearly with a free trial, Family yearly and Lifetime.
