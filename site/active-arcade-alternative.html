An Active Arcade alternative that runs in your browser

Updated 27 October 2026 · 4 min read

Active Arcade is a free app from NEX Team Inc. that turns your body into the controller of short games on an iPhone or iPad. Its App Store listing shows version 3.11, from 19 December 2022 [1]. BodyArcade is a set of 11 short camera games that runs in a browser instead. This page puts the two side by side and names the source and the day of every fact about Active Arcade.

Where Active Arcade is the better choice

  • Everything is free. The listing says there are no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscription [1]. BodyArcade keeps 3 of its 11 games for Pro.
  • Made for an iPhone or iPad on a TV. It is an app, and the listing suggests AirPlay or HDMI for the living room [1].
  • Many players have rated it. 4.7 from 1,133 ratings on the US App Store [1]. BodyArcade is new and has none yet.

Where BodyArcade is the better choice

  • No iPhone or iPad needed. It runs in the browser of an Android phone, a Windows or Mac computer, an iPhone or an iPad.
  • Nothing to download. Open the page, allow the camera and play. The camera video is read on your device and never uploaded.
  • Seated and quiet versions. Every game can be played from a chair, and 10 of 11 without jumping or stamping.
  • Friends far away. Live races in Squat Flap, Knee Sprint and Reaction Tag, in a room of 2 to 4 players, and ghost races against a recording.
  • Updated. The last release was on 27 October 2026, and the release notes list every change.

What NEX makes now

NEX Team Inc., the company behind Active Arcade, now sells Nex Playground, a games console for the TV. Its Play Pass costs $89 for 12 months or $49 for 3 months, and 5 games come with the console without a Play Pass [2]. If you want a box under the TV and a large library, it is worth a look; if you want to start on a device you already own, BodyArcade needs nothing more.

Questions people ask

Is there a free alternative to Active Arcade?

BodyArcade is one: 8 of its 11 games are free, with no ads and no account, and it runs in a browser instead of an app. Pro adds the other 3 games and more; the prices page has the details.

Can I play on an Android phone or a computer?

BodyArcade, yes: it runs in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari on a phone, tablet, laptop or desktop. For this page we read only Active Arcade’s App Store listing, which is for iPhone and iPad.

Can two people play together?

Both have two-player play. In Active Arcade two people stand side by side in front of one device [1]. In BodyArcade two people can share one camera on a big screen, and friends in other places can race live in a room of 2 to 4.

Is my camera video uploaded?

Not in BodyArcade: your device turns the video into 33 body points on the spot, and the video is never uploaded, recorded or saved. See the privacy policy.
