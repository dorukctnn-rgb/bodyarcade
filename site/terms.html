Who we are

BodyArcade (bodyarcade.com and the BodyArcade Android app) is made by Doruk Çetin, an individual who trades as Peak Apps Studio in Türkiye. In these terms “we” means him. You can reach us at info@peakappsstudio.com.

What BodyArcade is

BodyArcade is a set of 11 short games that you play with your body. A camera, your webcam or the front camera of your phone, reads your moves on your device. You can play in your browser or in the Android app. Keyboard or touch controls are there for games that allow them, if you cannot do the movements or have no camera.

The free plan has 8 games (Squat Flap, Knee Sprint, Star Catch, Firework Jacks, Twist Turbine, Summit Climb, Pose Wall and Road Run), their seated and quiet versions, the Easy and Normal difficulties, and no ads. Road Run has 10 worlds, and 3 of them are free. You can play each Pro game (Pad Work, Reaction Tag and Limbo Bend) once for free.

Pro adds the Pro games, the other 7 Road Run worlds, the Hard and Expert difficulties and the games we add later, with the plans below. The games we add later are included while a Pro monthly, Pro yearly or Family yearly membership is active, and on the Lifetime plan.

We keep BodyArcade running and improve it, so we may add, change or remove games and features. If we remove something that is a main part of a plan you paid for, we will try to replace it with something of similar value. Your rights as a consumer under the law are not affected. Online features need a network connection and depend on services of other companies (Cloudflare, Gumroad, Google), so we cannot promise that they are always available.

Play safely

The games ask you to squat, jump, twist, reach, bend and punch. You play at your own risk and at your own pace.

  • Clear about 2 metres around you, and play on a floor that is not slippery.
  • Check with a doctor first if you are pregnant, have a heart or joint condition, have not exercised for a while, or feel pain when you move.
  • Stop if you feel dizzy, short of breath or a sharp pain.
  • Children play with an adult nearby.
  • Every game has a seated version and 10 of 11 can be played quietly, without jumping or stamping. Use them when standing or jumping is a problem.

Not medical advice

BodyArcade does not measure your heart rate and gives no medical advice. Calorie figures are estimates from the weight you enter and how long you moved. Camera tracking can miss or misread a move, especially in poor light.

Who may use BodyArcade

Anyone can play on their own device. The online features (friends, rooms, public lobbies and weekly boards) are for people aged 16 and older. Under 16 there is no online profile at all: the games work on the device, and the app sends nothing about the player to the friends service. From 16 to 17 the profile is limited to a generated name and preset messages, with no video and no typed text. If you are under 18, please use BodyArcade with the knowledge of a parent or guardian, and ask them before you buy anything.

The app asks your age group once, before you make an online profile. Please give the true one: it decides what you may share and with whom, and it protects younger players. Saying you are older than you are, to get features meant for adults, breaks these terms.

Playing with others

An online profile is optional and has no password. Before the app makes one it asks you to tick a box that says you agree to these terms and to the community rules below, and it keeps the version of the terms you agreed to with the profile. Keep your recovery code safe: with your friend code it brings your profile back, and anyone who has both can take the profile over.

  • Names. Players aged 18 and older choose a display name of up to 20 characters. Players aged 16 to 17 get a generated name. A name must not pretend to be someone else, carry personal details or be rude.
  • Friend codes and links. Share them only with people you want to hear from. A friend request needs the other person to accept it.
  • Rooms. A room holds 2 to 4 players. The host picks the game, and in a private room the host can remove a player.
  • Public lobbies. You meet players of your own age group. They see your display name and a skeleton in your colour, and can send preset messages and reactions. Nobody you do not know receives typed text or video from you.
  • Typed chat exists only in private rooms where every player is 18 or older. Video between friends is optional, off by default, and works only in such a room when both friends switch it on.
  • Do not record, capture or share another person’s video or messages without their agreement.
  • When you race a recording or a practice partner (a bot), the app says so. We do not show a bot as a person, and we do not invent players for lobbies or boards.

Community rules: conduct, fair play and the boards

Please do not:

  • harass, threaten, bully or discriminate against anyone, or post sexual, hateful or violent content;
  • put personal details, your own or another person’s, into a name or a message, or try to get around the chat filter;
  • send spam, advertising or links;
  • use BodyArcade to seek contact with players under 18. The app does not let an adult send a friend request to a player under 18, and adults must not look for ways around that;
  • use scripts, modified apps or other tools to fake your moves or your scores, trade or boost scores, or use a bug to your advantage (please report bugs to us instead);
  • report people in bad faith.

The weekly boards list real players only and carry no prizes. The server checks that a score is possible for the time a round took, but it cannot replay your game, so a score is not proof. We can remove a score or hide a profile from a board.

Reports, mutes, suspensions and bans

You can report or block any player you meet. Every report goes into a queue that we read ourselves, and reports about serious reasons come first. Automatic steps protect other players until we have looked: when 3 different players report the same player within 24 hours, that player’s typed text is muted for 24 hours, and when 6 different players report them within 7 days, or 2 do for a serious reason within a day, public lobbies are closed to them for 3 days.

We may warn, mute, suspend or ban a profile, or remove a name or a score, when these rules are broken, and at once in serious cases. A ban ends the online features; local play and a paid plan stay. If you think we got it wrong, write to info@peakappsstudio.com and we will look again.
