What the game is

BodyArcade is a running game in your browser that you control with your body through your camera, or with the keyboard. The free plan gives you five runs a day in the Alps, Desert and Forest worlds, on Easy and Medium, with the daily goal, achievements and friend challenges.

Play safely

The game asks you to jump, squat, duck and lean. Clear the space around you, play on a floor that is not slippery, warm up first and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath. If you have a health condition, are pregnant or have not exercised for a while, check with a doctor before you play. Younger players should play with an adult nearby. Calorie figures are estimates based on the weight you enter and the moves you make. They are not medical advice or a medical measurement.
