A Ring Fit Adventure alternative you can play in a browser

Updated 26 October 2026 · 4 min read

Ring Fit Adventure is one of the best-loved fitness games ever made. It also needs a Nintendo Switch, its Joy-Con controllers, and the Ring-Con and Leg Strap that come in the box. If you do not own a Switch, or you want something you can start on a laptop you already have, you will want a different kind of game. BodyArcade is our answer to that. It is a set of short camera games and makes no attempt to copy Ring Fit. Here is how they compare.

Where Ring Fit Adventure is stronger

  • Resistance. Squeezing and pulling the Ring-Con adds a strength element that a camera cannot give you.
  • Variety. It has a large library of exercises, from squats and planks to yoga-style poses.
  • Story and progression. The adventure mode gives you a reason to come back for weeks.
  • No framing. The Joy-Con tracks you wherever you stand and in any light.

Where BodyArcade is stronger

  • Nothing to buy. It runs on the laptop, desktop, tablet or phone you already have.
  • Quick to start. Open the page, allow the camera and play. The first visit adds a welcome tour of about two minutes. There is nothing to install and no account to make.
  • Short rounds. A round lasts from 90 seconds to 7 minutes, so it works as a desk break or a quick warm-up.
  • Seated and quiet. Every game has a seated version, and 10 of 11 can be played without jumping or stamping.
  • Play together. Two people can share one camera on a big screen, and friends can join an online room of two to four players.

Setting up a camera-based workout

  • Distance. Stand where the camera sees your head to your ankles: 2 to 3 metres from a laptop or 1.5 to 2 metres from an upright phone.
  • Light. Face a window or a lamp. Strong light behind you makes tracking less reliable.
  • Stability. Put the laptop or phone on a table or shelf so it does not move while you do.
  • Footwear and floor. Trainers on a floor that is not slippery, with about 2 metres clear around you.

Frequently asked questions

Is BodyArcade a replacement for Ring Fit Adventure?

Not exactly. Ring Fit Adventure is a long role-playing game with resistance exercises that use the Ring-Con. BodyArcade is a set of short camera games for legs, arms, core and balance. It suits people who want quick, active games without buying a console or accessories.

Do I need any accessories for BodyArcade?

No. BodyArcade uses the camera on your laptop, desktop, tablet or phone. Pose detection runs on your device, and the video stays there.

Which is the better workout?

They train different things. Ring Fit adds resistance through the Ring-Con and has a wide range of strength and yoga-style moves. BodyArcade is short rounds of whole-body movement without resistance. Many people will enjoy both.

A round takes a couple of minutes and needs nothing but a camera.
