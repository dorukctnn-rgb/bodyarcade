Exercise games for PC that run in your browser

Updated 29 October 2026 · 5 min read

BodyArcade is a set of 11 short exercise games for a PC or a laptop with a webcam. Open the page, allow the camera and move: squat, jump, punch, twist, reach. There is no download and no controller. 8 of the games are free.

What a PC needs

  • A camera the browser can open. The one in a laptop lid or on top of a monitor is enough, and so is a webcam you plug in.
  • A current browser. Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. The game opens as a web page, so there is nothing to download and no installer to run.
  • No account. You can play the free games without an e-mail address or a password.
  • One small fetch the first time. The game fetches its pose model, about 6 MB, and keeps it on your device. The model runs on your graphics chip when the browser can use one and on the processor when it cannot.
  • Some room. About 2 metres clear around you if you play standing. Nothing else: no weights, bands or sensors.

Your PC turns each camera frame into 33 body points on the spot. The video is never uploaded, recorded or saved.

Games you can play at a desk

Star Catch, Pad Work, Twist Turbine and Limbo Bend need only your head down to your hips in view, about 1 metre from the camera, so you can play them seated at a desk. When the camera sees only your head and shoulders, the camera check offers to play at the desk, as in the picture.

There is a ready break for this. Desk break is 2 seated games, Twist Turbine and Star Catch, for about 6 minutes with a rest of 10 seconds between them. It is the one workout plan that is free. Every game, including the standing ones, also has a seated version in Settings.

Games that need floor space

7 of the 11 games need your whole body in view, from your head to your ankles. Stand 2 to 3 metres from a laptop. Tilt the screen back until the camera sees your feet, and put the light in front of you or beside you, not behind you. How webcam fitness games work has the full set-up for a room.

What a webcam game cannot do

  • It cannot add resistance. A camera cannot make you push against anything, so these games are bodyweight movement and cardio. For a strength programme, use weights.
  • It can miss a move. One camera reads big moves of big body parts well. Fine hand positions, very fast moves and anything hidden behind a chair or a desk are harder. When a move does not count, the results screen says why.
  • It is not medical advice. It does not measure heart rate. Check with a doctor first if you are pregnant, have a heart or joint condition, or feel pain when you move. Stop if you feel dizzy or a sharp pain.

Questions people ask

Is there a download for PC?

No. BodyArcade runs in the browser of a laptop or desktop, so there is no installer and no file to keep up to date. Open the page, allow the camera and play.

Which browsers work?

A current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, on a laptop or desktop with a camera. The first time, the browser asks if the page may use the camera.

Can I play without a webcam?

Games that allow it can be played with the keyboard or by touch. Those rounds are practice: they are not kept in your records, your active days or your calories.

Do I need a gaming PC?

BodyArcade lists no required parts. The camera reading runs on the graphics chip when your browser can use one and on the processor when it cannot. Open the first game and see how it runs on your machine.

Is my camera video uploaded?

No. Your PC turns the video into 33 body points on the spot, and the video is never uploaded, recorded or saved. See the privacy policy.

How much does it cost?

8 of the 11 games are free, with their seated and quiet versions and no ads. Pro adds the other 3 games and more. The Pro page and the prices page have the plans.
