The controller

Five moves. That's all of it.

Every obstacle on the road is cleared by one of these. The game reads them from your hips, head and shoulders with on-device pose tracking, calibrated to your own standing height.

No webcam handy? Pick Play with keyboard: ↑ or Space to jump, ↓ or Shift to crouch, ← → to lean. On a phone, swipe.

How a run works

From tab to sweat in a minute.

There's no install and no sign-up. Open the game, pick a world, let the camera find you and start moving.

Pick a world and a pace

Alps, Desert and Forest are free on Easy or Medium. Type a name if you want your best runs labelled, and your weight if you want the calorie estimate to fit you.

Let the camera see you

Step back until your head and hips fit in the outline, stand still for a second, and the game learns your standing height. That calibration is what makes jumps and squats register for every body.

Run for 90 seconds

Obstacles come down the road and a ring fills as each one gets close. Make your move when it turns yellow. Three misses end the run; finishing the full ninety earns a bonus.

Privacy

The camera watches. Nobody else does.

Here's exactly what happens to your data. The privacy page has the full details.

Video stays on your device

The pose model (MediaPipe) runs inside your browser. Camera frames are never uploaded, recorded or stored.

No account, no sign-up

Your name, weight, runs and achievements are saved in this browser. There is no server-side profile to create or delete.

Only counts, not people

Plausible counts page views and a few button presses without cookies. If you go Pro, your license key is checked with Gumroad and nothing else.

Questions

Before you press play

Do I need to download anything?

No. BodyArcade runs in your web browser. Open the game page, allow the camera, stand back and play. There is no app, no account and nothing to install. You can add it to your home screen if you like, but you don't have to.

What equipment do I need?

A laptop or desktop with a webcam, or a phone with a front camera, and about two metres of clear floor so the camera can see you from your head down to your hips.

Is my camera video uploaded or recorded?

No. The pose model runs inside your browser on your device. Camera frames never leave your computer and are never recorded or stored. Your scores and settings are saved in your browser only.

Can I play without a camera?

Yes. Choose Play with keyboard: arrow keys or Space to jump, down arrow or Shift to crouch, left and right arrows to lean. On a phone you can swipe. Camera play is the workout; keyboard play is for trying the game.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, with a recent Chrome or Safari. Prop the phone up at about hip height, step back until your head and hips are in the outline, and play. A laptop gives the camera a wider view and is the easiest setup.

What does the free version include?

Five runs a day in the Alps, Desert and Forest worlds on Easy or Medium, with the daily goal, achievements and friend challenges. Pro unlocks unlimited runs, all ten worlds, Hard and Insane difficulty, every runner look and full history.

How do I activate Pro after buying?

Gumroad emails you a license key after checkout. Open the game, press Pro, paste the key and press Activate. The key is remembered in that browser, so do it once on each device you play on.

How accurate is the calorie count?

It's an estimate based on a standard MET value for moderate exercise, the body weight you enter and the moves you make. Use it to compare your own sessions, not as a medical measurement.
