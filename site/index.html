How a run works

From tab to sweat in a minute.

There's no install and no sign-up. Open the game, stand back and start moving.

Allow the camera

Step back until the camera sees you from your head to your hips. The video is processed on your device and never uploaded.

Pick a world

Alps, Desert and Forest are free on Easy or Medium. Pro adds seven more worlds and the Hard and Insane difficulties.

Run for 90 seconds

Obstacles come at you and your body clears them. When you finish, you get your score, distance, best combo and an estimate of calories burned.

Questions

Before you press play

Do I need to download anything?

No. BodyArcade runs in your web browser. Open the page, allow the camera, and play. You can add it to your home screen if you like, but you don't have to.

What equipment do I need?

A laptop or desktop webcam, or a phone's front camera, and enough room to step back so the camera sees you from your head down to your hips. A couple of metres of clear floor is enough for most rooms.

Is my camera video uploaded or recorded?

No. Pose detection runs in your browser on your device. Camera frames are never uploaded or stored. Only your finished run results (name, score, distance, world) go to the leaderboard.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It runs in Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android. Prop the phone up on a shelf or against a wall so the front camera can see you from head to hips.

What does the free version include?

Five runs a day in the Alps, Desert and Forest worlds on Easy or Medium, plus live races and the leaderboard. Pro unlocks unlimited runs, all ten worlds and the Hard and Insane difficulties.

How accurate is the calorie count?

It's an estimate based on standard MET values for each movement and the body weight you enter. Use it to compare your own sessions, not as a medical measurement.
