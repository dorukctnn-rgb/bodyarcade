Delete your account and data

Last updated 26 October 2026

BodyArcade is made by Doruk Çetin, trading as Peak Apps Studio. This page covers bodyarcade.com and the BodyArcade Android app. It explains how to delete the online profile you made in BodyArcade, what that erases and when, and what stays and why. The privacy policy has the full list of what is kept.

In short

  • You delete your profile yourself, in the app. Friends, then My profile, then Delete my profile. Everything the profile holds on our servers is erased at once.
  • You can take a copy first. Download my data gives you one file.
  • Lost the device? Bring the profile back with your friend code and your recovery code, then delete it. Without them, write to info@peakappsstudio.com with your friend code.
  • A few things stay for a while, and the page says which and for how long: reports that protect other players, the safety log, payment records and Pro license records.
  • You do not need a profile to play. Without one, your settings and results are on your device only.

Delete your profile in the app

  1. Open Friends, then My profile.
  2. Under Your data, choose Download my data if you want a copy first. It is one file with your profile, friends, requests, blocks, weekly scores and synced records, the number of reports you filed and that were filed about you, and the version of the terms you agreed to. The file is saved on your device and is not sent anywhere.
  3. Choose Delete my profile. The app asks “Delete your profile?”. Confirm with Delete my profile.

Deleting erases the profile, its devices, friends, requests, blocks, presence, scores and synced records at once, closes your connections, tells your friends’ devices that you are gone and removes the profile from the device you used. It cannot be undone. The games, settings and results on your device stay.

The same two buttons work on the web and in the Android app, and also when the profile has been suspended or banned.

If you no longer have the device

When you made the profile, the app showed a recovery code once. If you saved it, you can bring the profile back on any browser or phone: open Friends, choose Make my profile, then I already have a profile, and enter your friend code and your recovery code. After that, delete the profile as above.

If you have neither the device nor the recovery code, write to info@peakappsstudio.com. Use the subject “Delete my BodyArcade profile” and give your friend code (it looks like BA-7K2Q4X). The friend code is how we find the profile, so we cannot act without it. It is on the Friends screen of the app, and in the recovery file the app offered to save. We may ask for something that shows the profile is yours. When we are sure, we erase the profile with the same steps as the app’s button. We answer within 30 days.

If you think a child under 16 has made an online profile, write to the same address and we will remove it. A profile that nobody uses is also erased by itself, 400 days after it was last used online.

What is erased, and when

DevicesThe public half of the key of each device that can sign for the profile.At once
Friends and requestsYour friendships (on both sides), requests you sent and received, and declined requests.At once

PresenceWhether you are online, in a room or in a game.At once

From the live database these records are gone as soon as you confirm. Cloudflare, which stores them for us, keeps a recovery history of that storage for up to 30 days, so a deleted record can still sit in that history for up to that long before it is gone for good.

What stays, and why

  • Reports. Reports in which you were involved stay for up to 90 days after we handled them, because they protect other players. A report about a profile that no longer exists goes 30 days after it was made. A report you filed loses its link to your profile, and keeps the reason, the room, your optional note, the player you reported and the saved chat lines. A report about you loses your display name and any note, and keeps the reason, the room, a random profile number that no longer belongs to anyone, and the saved chat lines, which still carry the display names and the words used in the room.
  • The safety log. What a moderator or an automatic rule did (mute, suspension, ban) and why is kept for 365 days. An entry about you carries the random profile number and the moderator’s note, not your name.
  • Rooms. A room you were in is deleted 10 minutes after the last player leaves, and in any case 24 hours after its last activity, together with its chat and results. Its entry in the list of rooms is hidden 15 minutes after the room stops updating, and removed 6 hours after its last update at the latest.
  • Pro license records. A Pro license is not tied to your profile, so deleting the profile does not touch it, and Pro keeps working. Our license service holds a fingerprint of the license key (or of the Google Play purchase token), the plan, its status and dates, and a random number for each device. A device gives its place back after 90 days unused, and a purchase that ended, with no device left, is forgotten 180 days after its last check. A running plan is kept while it runs. If you want one of these records removed earlier, write to info@peakappsstudio.com.
  • Payment records. Payments go through Gumroad on the web and Google Play in the Android app, which keep their own records under their own policies. As the seller we receive your e-mail address and the details of your order, and we keep them for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Subscriptions

Deleting your profile does not cancel a subscription. On the web, cancel in your Gumroad library (choose BodyArcade, then Manage membership). In the Android app, cancel in Google Play subscriptions. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. The terms explain refunds.

Data on your device

Your settings, results and ghost runs are kept on your device only, whether or not you have a profile. Settings has a button, Clear data on this device, that clears them. It does not delete an online profile, because the profile screen has its own button for that. Clearing the site’s data in your browser removes everything on the device, including the key of your profile, so delete the profile first or keep your recovery code.

Questions

Write to info@peakappsstudio.com. How BodyArcade handles data is in the privacy policy, where “Your choices and your rights” lists the rights you have under the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the Turkish KVKK.
