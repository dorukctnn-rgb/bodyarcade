The Court: toys on a puck, in daylight.

BodyArcade's 3D is a small diorama: a round sports floor with painted lines, one soft clay-like player, a few props from the game, one key light from the upper left. It is our own language, built from primitives, so every world stays light on a phone and nothing looks like a stock asset.

Release 1 catalogue art

One world colour per game, the player on the puck in the game's key pose. Rendered by node design/tools/render-tiles.mjs to 800 x 600 and 400 x 300 WebP.

Rules for every world

  • The player is always the Mover: ball head in flush, ink body (chalk on night worlds), sun band on the left wrist, sky band on the right. No faces, no clothes, no mascot.
  • Matte materials, roughness 0.5 to 0.85, no metal, no glow except stars and fireworks. Neutral tone mapping so flush stays flush.
  • One key light upper left front, a sky and ground fill, a warm rim from behind. Soft blob shadows on phones; real shadows only on the high tier.
  • Field of view 27 to 30 degrees, a three-quarter view from slightly above. The puck may bleed off the frame; the player never does.
  • Phone budget: under 60 draw calls and 30 000 triangles per scene, no post-processing; vignette and grain are CSS.
  • Assets: everything in release 1 is procedural. Later worlds may use CC0 packs from Kenney and Poly Haven only, credited in the licence list. Quaternius is not used since its licence changed.
