Rules

  • No rankings, awards, prices, free-trial text, player counts or counts of games in a caption or in a picture the captions point at. The catalogue grows every week, so a caption never says how many games there are, and the games screen hides the line that does.
  • Captions are sentence case, one idea each, readable at thumbnail size; the flush underline marks the key words. English follows core/ui/i18n/en.ts and Turkish follows tr.ts, in the same vocabulary. All captions are in captions.json.
  • Only what the app does today appears, as the Android app: two players on one camera is never offered there, so it is not in any image. The skeleton replay of a miss is not in the app yet, so no caption mentions it; the reasons are the ones the app already gives.
  • The game picture is a real round of Squat Flap played with the keyboard until the score is up, with all three feathers left. The framing check shows the practice session's stand-in figure for a person. The sample friends in the room are sample names, and the player's own name in it is replaced by a neutral one.
  • No line of text is cut in half by an edge. A screen that scrolls (the room, the settings, the games) is photographed at the few pixels of scroll where the bottom edge, or the bar over it, falls between two lines; where no scroll does it, the picture of a tablet ends up to 56 CSS px higher, and the device in the image is as tall as its picture. Nothing is stretched. The capture tool prints what it did and fails the render when a line is still cut.
  • Each language gets its own capture and its own render, never text pasted over an image of another language.
  • The Play images are in export/en and export/tr. export/feature-graphic.png at the top of the folder is the older feature graphic that packages/web/content/build.ts copies into the site as its share image; it stays byte for byte as it was until the site is rebuilt from the new one.
  • Re-render after any UI change or new game: node C:/Users/doruk/apps/_tools/e2e-slot.mjs run -- node design/tools/render-store.mjs (also --lang tr, --only phone, --shots games). Icons: node design/tools/render-brand.mjs.
