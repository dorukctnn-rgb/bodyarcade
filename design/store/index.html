Google Play graphics

Daylight and flat colour in a category that is dark, neon and loud. Every image is rendered from the real product: the 3D court from onboarding and real screens from the UI kit, so the listing never promises something the app does not show.

Feature graphic

1024 x 500, 24-bit PNG. Headline and hero sit inside the middle 80 %, nothing important near the edges. The night version is for promotions and dark placements.

Icon

The flat mark ships as the icon: a person made of a stick and a ball, standing on the line. It reads at 48 px where a 3D render turns to mush, and it matches the favicon and the logo. 3D lives in the feature graphic and the screenshots.

Phone screenshots

1080 x 1920, in this order. One promise per image, a short caption that is true, the real screen rising from the bottom.

Rules

  • No rankings, awards, prices or player counts in any image. No "free" badge on Pro features.
  • Captions are sentence case, one idea each, readable at thumbnail size; the flush underline marks the key words.
  • Only features that ship in this release appear. Two players on one camera is a big-screen feature, so it is not in the phone set.
  • The Turkish set reuses the same layouts with the Turkish strings; each locale gets its own render, never text pasted over an English image.
  • Re-render after any UI change: node design/tools/render-store.mjs. Icons: node design/tools/render-brand.mjs.
