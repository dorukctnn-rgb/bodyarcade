A person on the line.

The mark is a player drawn like a joystick: the ball is the head, the stick is the body, the base is the line every game measures against. Flush for the ball, ink for the person, daylight around it.

Logo

Use the horizontal logo in headers, the stacked one where space is square, the mark alone at icon sizes. Keep clear space of one ball width all round.

Colour

Flush is the hero: the main button, the command plate in games and the line. Text on flush is ink, never white (5.5 to 1). Body regions are shown by pictograms, not by a rainbow.

The line

Each game measures a move against a line fitted to your body: squat depth, knee height, reach. A miss is explained against the line, and the one second replay draws the skeleton with it.

Do not

  • No neon glows, gradients on the logo, or outlines around the mark.
  • No mascot. The player is the person in front of the camera.
  • No white text on flush, no flush text on night court below 24 px.
  • No capitals for headlines; sentence case, short and true.
