### Real people in motion (the game tiles, the body areas, the picker, "how it works", the form coach, the challenge of the day, the welcome tour)

`packages/web/src/motion.ts` lists them. For each id below the files are `design/art/clips/mo-<id>-<shape>.mp4` (H.264), `.webm` (VP9) and `.json` (the body line, below) and the posters `design/art/hero/mo-<id>-<shape>.avif`, `.webp`, where `<shape>` is `tile` (640x480) and, where the tour or "how it works" uses it, `tall` (720x900). What we did to every clip: a 2 to 5 s part cut where the move repeats, cropped round the person (a clip shot upright gets black sides that fade into the picture), turned grey (10 % colour left) and darkened with a gamma to the hero's level, a light vignette, no sound; the loop's seam dissolves over 4 frames, or the part plays forward and back (marked "forward and back"). The poster is the clip's first frame with our body line drawn on it. Every page below was read on 19 Nov 2026; none is marked "Restricted" or AI-generated. The people are example footage, not BodyArcade players, and no name or quote is put on anyone.

## Our own pictures

| `design/art/hero/cap-<game>-480.webp`, `cap-<game>-960.webp` (11 games) | Frames of each game in play, rendered by our own game engine in a headless browser (`node tools/gfx-shots/run.ts <out> --clean --sizes=1280x800 --quality=high`, keyboard input), cut to 4:3. |

Generator: `_media/ba-sports-work/logo/make.py` (not in the repo). |
| `design/brand/og-site.png` (served as `/assets/brand/og.png`) | The site's link-preview card, 1024 x 500: the new logo, the headline in Anton and the mark, rendered from HTML in headless Edge. The Play feature graphic (`design/store/export/en/feature-graphic.png`) is unchanged and stays the store's. |
| `packages/web/src/hero-pose/arena-*.json`, `design/art/clips/mo-*.json` | The body points of the hero clips and of the real people in motion, read by MediaPipe's pose landmarker (`pose_landmarker_heavy`, Apache-2.0, run once offline through our own tool: `tools/hero-pose/extract-still.ts`, every frame of the very file the page plays read on its own (IMAGE mode, no smoothing over time), then `compact.ts --per-frame --smooth`, a filter centred on each frame so it never trails a move). Only the points ship; the model does not. One pose per frame of the clip, so the line matches every frame. |
