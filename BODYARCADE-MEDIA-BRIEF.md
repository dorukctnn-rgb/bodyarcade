# BodyArcade: media brief

The redesigned site uses only real material: gameplay captured from the live game in all 10 worlds, the in-game camera and menu screens, and a phone-sized gameplay frame. What it can't show yet is a **real person playing**, the one thing a first-time visitor most wants to see before allowing camera access.

These three assets would add the most. They are listed in priority order. None of them should be generated or staged with stock footage: the value is that they are real.

---

## 1. Split-screen play clip (highest impact)

**Where:** Hero, replacing the still gameplay screen (`site/assets/screens/play-hud.webp`). Also usable as the social preview video.

**What:** 10–12 seconds with a person on the left, filmed from behind or beside the laptop, doing a squat, a jump and a lean. On the right is the screen recording of the same moments, with obstacles cleared in sync.

- **Capture:** Film the player with a phone at 1080p/60 on a tripod, from roughly the laptop camera's height, 2–3 m away. At the same time, record the screen with OBS at 1920×1080/60. Sync the two in the edit using a jump.
- **Location:** A real living room or bedroom with daylight from a window. Everyday workout clothes. The player should look like a customer, not a model.
- **Consent:** Get a signed release from anyone on camera, and a guardian's consent for minors.
- **Delivery:**
  - `site/assets/video/play-split.mp4` (H.264, CRF 24, 1600×800, no audio, ≤ 4 MB)
  - `site/assets/video/play-split.webm` (VP9, CRF 34)
  - `site/assets/video/play-split.jpg` (poster, first frame)
- **Loop:** Start and end on the player standing still in front of an empty road.

## 2. Three real session photos

**Where:**
- The "How a run works" steps.
- The Live races section background, replacing the City world still.

**What:** Stills of people actually playing: a squat in front of a laptop on a coffee table, a phone propped on a shelf, two friends racing on two devices. Shoot them from behind or to the side so the screen is visible.

- **Delivery:** 2400 px wide JPEG masters. The site uses WebP at 1400 and 700 px.
- **Framing:** Keep a 16:10 crop safe. The step cards crop to 4:3.

## 3. 60-second real-run screen capture

**Where:** YouTube or social, embedded in a future "How it works" page. It's also useful as a video sitemap entry.

**What:** One uninterrupted Medium run in the Sunset world, captured in demo-free play with the camera on, showing the countdown, obstacles, the score climbing and the game-over summary.

---

### What not to use

- Generated images of people exercising.
- Stock "family jumping in living room" photos.
- Any clip from other fitness games.

The page's trust comes from the fact that everything on it is the real product.
