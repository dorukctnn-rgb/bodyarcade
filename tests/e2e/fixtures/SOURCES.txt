Fake camera feeds for the e2e tests (Chromium --use-file-for-fake-video-capture). Landmarks only are derived
from them; no video leaves the test machine.

standing-640x360.mjpeg
  Source: Wikimedia Commons, "Conditioning Drill 3- Alternate Staggered Squat Jump.webm", U.S. Army video,
  public domain (work of the U.S. federal government). Seconds 2 to 8, scaled to 640x360, 15 fps, MJPEG.
  Made with: ffmpeg -ss 2 -t 6 -i <source>.webm -vf "fps=15,scale=640:360" -c:v mjpeg -q:v 5 -an standing-640x360.mjpeg

two-standing-640x360.mjpeg
  Two people side by side for the two-player tests (mode 'local2'): the same public-domain Army clip as above,
  the centre 320 px of each frame twice, the right copy mirrored and 37 frames (about 2.5 s) later, 6 s, 15 fps.
  Made with: ffmpeg -f mjpeg -framerate 15 -i standing-640x360.mjpeg -stream_loop 1 -f mjpeg -framerate 15
  -i standing-640x360.mjpeg -filter_complex "[0:v]crop=320:360:160:0,setpts=PTS-STARTPTS[a];[1:v]trim=start_frame=37,
  setpts=PTS-STARTPTS,crop=320:360:160:0,hflip[b];[a][b]hstack=inputs=2:shortest=1" -c:v mjpeg -q:v 5 -an
  -f mjpeg two-standing-640x360.mjpeg
