How It Works

Three steps to your
best workout

No setup, no equipment. Just open your browser, allow camera access, and start moving.

BodyArcade uses your camera to track your body in real time. No data is stored — all processing happens locally in your browser.

Features

Built Different

🤖
MediaPipe AI Tracking
33-point body skeleton tracked at 30fps. Joint angles are measured to verify every rep with real biomechanical precision.
✨
WebGL + Bloom Rendering
Three.js with UnrealBloom post-processing. Neon obstacles, bioluminescent forests, and cinematic ACES tone mapping.
🔥
Real Calorie Science
MET (Metabolic Equivalent of Task) formula adapted for each exercise. Personalized to your weight for accurate results.
🔒
100% Private
Camera feed never leaves your device. All AI pose detection runs locally in your browser. Zero data collection.
🏆
Global Leaderboard
Compete with players worldwide. Scores ranked by points, calories burned, distance and best combo streak.
🎵
Adaptive Audio
Web Audio API generative soundtrack. Bass drone, atmospheric pads and LFO modulation that responds to your movement.
